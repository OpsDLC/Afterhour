Listen to the rain?

      
Afterhour can let real rain drive the droplets. Sound from your microphone is analysed
        on this device only: loudness, rhythm and how much bass or treble there is.

      
    
        
  • No speech recognition.
    • 
        
  • Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded.
    • 
        
  • The microphone is never played back through your speakers.
    • 
      

      
Your browser will ask for permission next. You can stop at any time by choosing another source.

Privacy

      
Everything runs in this page. Audio is analysed on-device with Web Audio and is never uploaded.
        Exported videos contain no original audio unless you tick the box when exporting.
