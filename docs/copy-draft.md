# Positioning and copy — draft (H4N-8)

Draft only. Nothing here is published. For the Product Marketer and Yunhan to rewrite freely.

## One line

**Afterhour — night rain on your words.**

Alternates:
- Let the rain read what you didn't send.
- Words, after hours, in the rain.

## Short description (≈ 160 chars)

Type a few lines and let night rain fall on them. Drops magnify and turn your words over, gather,
and slide away. Listens to real rain, on your device only.

## Landing / about

> It is late. The city is quiet except for the rain.
>
> Afterhour puts your words where the rain can reach them. Each drop is a small lens: it lifts a
> letter, turns it upside down, holds it for a moment. Drops gather. When they grow heavy enough,
> they let go and slide away, and the words behind them are a little wetter, a little clearer.
>
> Leave it on as a quiet companion, or hold your phone to the window and let the real rain decide.
> The sound stays on your device — Afterhour listens only to how loud the rain is and how it falls,
> never to what anyone says. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
>
> When a moment feels right, save a short clip. It has no sound unless you choose to include it.

## Tone notes

- Night rain (夜雨) persona: quiet, unhurried, a little melancholy, never gloomy.
- No glass, window or weather-app imagery in copy or visuals — the pane is only a concept.
- Avoid "AI", "immersive", "experience". Short sentences. Lowercase is fine for the default words.
- Chinese line for bilingual share cards (draft): **把没说出口的话，交给夜雨。**
